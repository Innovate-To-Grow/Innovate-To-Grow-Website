download Download Import Template

Download the Excel template, fill in member information, then upload. A file from Export to Excel works too: its Member UUID column matches each row to its member.

How an import works

  • Upload the sheet. Nothing is written yet: every row is checked against the members already stored, and you get a review.
  • Review what each row would do. Rows that are ready (new members, or updates of a member matched by primary email or Member UUID) are applied unless you tick Skip. A row that needs a decision (for example, its address is someone's secondary email, or the name differs) is skipped until you choose what to do.
  • Risky changes are never made unless you tick them one by one: removing an address or phone number, re-subscribing, verifying or un-verifying an email address (a verified address is a working sign-in), (de)activating, staff status.
  • Confirm by typing import. Every row is written in one go; if anything the review showed changed in the meantime, nothing is written and you review those rows again.
  • A blank cell, or a missing column, keeps the stored value. Addresses and numbers added to an existing member start unverified. No email or SMS is sent.
  • Primary Email is the only required column; a new member also needs First and Last Name. TRUE/FALSE cells accept TRUE, FALSE, Yes, No, Y, N, 1 and 0; other text is ignored. Last Updated and Primary Expired are not imported.
  • Phone Verified is ignored: imported phone numbers are saved unverified, and numbers already stored keep their status. Members verify their own numbers with a one-time code sent by SMS.
  • One import takes up to {{ max_rows }} rows. An import left in review is deleted after {{ lifetime_hours }} hours.
